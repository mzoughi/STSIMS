Physics model: The battery is represented by an ideal emf source in series with internal resistance. The wires also have resistance. Every element lies in one series loop, so the same current passes through the battery, wire, and all three subsystem resistors. The terminal voltage is less than the emf when current flows because some voltage is lost inside the battery.
