Model limits  —  The simulation uses the ideal thin-lens, paraxial approximation; it does not model thickness, wavelength, energy loss, or spherical aberration. The opening is limited to 2R for a possible spherical surface. Image distance uses the sign convention: positive is a real image after the lens, negative is a virtual image before it. At nrel = 1, the lens has no refractive power.
